Authorization to Release Health Information

HIPAA authorization · 45 CFR 164.508

Date of birth\c_dob\
Personal representative signing for the patient, if any: name and authority

1. Who may release my information

Any doctor, hospital, clinic, pharmacy, ambulance or emergency service, therapist, or other health care provider, and any health plan, that has treated me or paid for my treatment.

2. Who may receive it

Morgan & Morgan, and the attorneys and staff working on my claim.

3. What may be released

My medical records and bills, including office and hospital notes, test results and imaging, prescriptions, and billing and payment records, from five years before the incident on \i_date\ to the present.

This authorization does not cover psychotherapy notes. Records about alcohol or drug treatment, mental health treatment, or HIV/AIDS testing or treatment are released only if I initial here: \c_init\

4. Why

At my request, so the Firm can evaluate and pursue my personal injury claim.

5. When this authorization ends

Two years after the date I sign it, or when my claim is closed, whichever comes first.

6. My rights

  • I may revoke this authorization at any time by writing to Morgan & Morgan. Revoking it does not affect information already released in reliance on it.
  • My health care providers and health plans may not condition my treatment, payment, enrollment, or eligibility for benefits on whether I sign this authorization.
  • Information released under this authorization may be released again by the person who receives it, and may then no longer be protected by federal privacy law.
  • I am entitled to a copy of this authorization. A copy or an electronic version is as valid as the original.
