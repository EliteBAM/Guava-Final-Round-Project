# Morgan and Morgan intake line: caller FAQ

Sample content for a software demonstration. Not issued by or affiliated with Morgan & Morgan. Facts about the firm
come from its public website (see Documents/Legal Intake Specialist Agent Notes/Research Findings.md, Key takeaway 9).
Questions about fees, percentages or whether to sign are answered in code (compliance.FEE_ANSWER) and aren't covered here.

## What happens after this call?
An attorney reviews the information you gave and decides whether the firm can take your case. If the firm can help,
an attorney and legal team are typically assigned within about a week.

## When will someone contact me?
Your case team reaches out after the attorney's review. If you need the firm sooner, you can call (877) 667-4265 or
use the contact form at forthepeople.com.

## What should I gather in the meantime?
Anything you have about the incident: photos of the scene, vehicles or injuries, the police report number, your
medical records and bills, your insurance cards, and the names and phone numbers of any witnesses.

## What documents will I receive?
A Statement of Client's Rights, which you should read in full, then the fee agreement and a form that lets the team
request your medical records. They arrive by email from DocuSign. You can sign whenever you're ready, and the
agreement is only final once a Morgan and Morgan attorney has signed it too.

## I didn't get the email with my documents.
It can take a few minutes, and it sometimes lands in a spam or junk folder. If it still hasn't arrived, the team can
send it again.

## What happens to the information I give you?
It's kept confidential and used by the firm to review your situation.

## The other driver's insurance company called me. What should I do?
That's a good question for an attorney, and one will be able to go over it with you. I'm not able to give legal advice.

## Should I see a doctor?
I can't give medical advice. If it's an emergency, call 911. Otherwise, please follow your doctor's guidance.

## How much is my case worth? Will I win?
Only an attorney can evaluate a case, and they'll do that after reviewing your information. I'm not able to give an
opinion on it.

## Where is the firm, and how can I reach it?
Morgan and Morgan is headquartered in Orlando, Florida. You can reach the firm any time, day or night, at
(877) 667-4265 or through forthepeople.com.
